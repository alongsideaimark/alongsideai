01 — What this is

A short explanation of what we collect, why, and what we never do with it.

Lantern Plan sells one thing: a personalized written plan that helps people — mostly non-technical professionals in the second half of their careers — choose and set up modern tools for the way they actually work. To write a plan that fits you, we need to know a little about you. This policy explains what we ask for, what we do with it, and how to tell us to delete it.

02 — What we collect

Only what we need to write your plan. Nothing for advertising.

We ask for your name, your email, and your answers to the questionnaire. Payment goes through Stripe — we never see your card number. That's it.
  • Information you give us. Your name, email, and answers to the questionnaire — how you work, the tools you already use, your goals, and any concerns about adopting new technology.
  • Payment confirmation from Stripe. When you pay, Stripe (our payment processor) handles your card entirely on their systems and tells us only that a payment succeeded, along with the email you used at checkout. We never see or store card numbers.
  • Emails you send us. If you reply to a delivery email or write with a question, we keep the correspondence so we can help you.
  • Basic technical logs. Our hosting providers keep standard server logs (things like page requests and IP addresses) to run and secure the site. We don't run advertising trackers or sell attention to anyone.
  • Nothing we don't need. We don't ask for Social Security numbers, bank credentials, or passwords — and the questionnaire will never require them. Please don't type them into it.
03 — Why we collect it

To write your plan, deliver it, and nothing else.

  • To write your plan. Every recommendation in your plan comes from what you told us in the questionnaire.
  • To deliver it and handle revisions. We email you a link to your plan, and we keep your answers on file so the two included revisions can account for what you originally said.
  • To honor the refund promise. Payment records let us process the 14-day refund if you ask for it.
  • To improve the service. Patterns across many plans — not your individual record — inform how we make plans better.
04 — Who sees your information

A very short list. Truly.

Us, and the handful of services it takes to run the product. Nobody else — no marketers, no partners, no data brokers. Ever.
  • Us. The principal of Lantern Plan and, in the future, a small number of vetted team members under written confidentiality.
  • The services that run the product. Stripe processes payments. Netlify hosts the site and stores questionnaire answers and plans. Anthropic provides the AI that drafts your plan (see section 08). Resend delivers our emails. PDFShift produces the archived PDF copy of your plan. Cloudflare sits in front of the site for security. Each is a reputable service with its own privacy protections, and we give them your information only to do the specific job listed here.
  • Law enforcement, if legally required. We'd tell you if this ever happened, unless the law prohibits us from doing so.
  • Nobody else. We don't sell, rent, or trade your information. Ever.
05 — How long we keep it

As long as it's useful to you — not a minute past.

We keep your questionnaire answers and your plan while your revision window is open and afterward, so your plan link keeps working and we can help if you come back with questions. Payment records are kept as long as tax and accounting rules require.

You can tell us to delete your information sooner. See the next section.

06 — Your rights

You own your information. Always.

  • Access. Ask us what we have on file and we'll send it to you within seven days.
  • Correction. If something's wrong, tell us and we'll fix it.
  • Deletion. Email us and we'll remove everything we're not required by law to keep.
  • Unsubscribe. Every email we send has an unsubscribe link. One click, no questions.

To exercise any of these, email mark@lanternplan.com with the request. No form to fill out.

07 — How we protect it

Reasonable care, no theater.

We store information in business-grade tools with two-factor authentication. We use strong passwords and keep our systems updated. We don't claim "bank-level" security because we aren't a bank — but we treat your information with the same discretion we'd want for our own.

If a data incident ever occurred that affected you, we'd tell you within 72 hours, explain what happened, and let you know what to do about it.

08 — How your plan is written

Your plan is drafted with AI. Your answers don't train it.

We're straightforward about this everywhere on the site: your plan is researched and drafted by AI, working from your questionnaire answers, then run through automated quality checks before it's sent. That's how a personalized, researched document can cost $79 instead of $2,000.

To do that, your answers are processed by Anthropic (the AI provider) through their business API. Under those business terms, your information is not used to train their models. Your answers and your finished plan are stored on our own systems, not theirs.

A person — Mark — can read any submission and any plan, and reviews plans that the quality checks flag. When you reply to a plan email, a person reads it.

09 — Changes to this policy

We'll tell you. Not bury it in an update.

If we materially change this policy, we'll email everyone who's bought a plan in the past year. The "last updated" date at the top will also change.

Lantern Plan ("we," "us," "our") is a service of Alongside AI LLC, a California limited liability company. We sell a personalized written plan that helps you choose and set up modern software and AI tools for the way you actually work. Buying a plan means you agree to these terms.

02 — What you're buying

One payment, one personalized plan — exactly what the pricing page says.

You pay $79 once. You answer a questionnaire. We send you a personalized plan. Two free revisions are included for 14 days. There is no subscription, no follow-up sales call, and no fine print beyond this page.
  • The plan. A personalized written document — which tools fit your situation, how to set them up step by step, what they cost, what to skip, and why. It's researched and drafted by AI from your questionnaire answers, then run through automated quality checks (see the Privacy Policy, section 08).
  • Delivery. By email, usually within the hour, as a link you can read in your browser and save as a PDF. If something goes wrong on our side, we'll tell you and make it right.
  • Two revisions, 14 days. If something in the plan doesn't fit — a tool you already use, a budget that should be different, a detail you forgot to mention — use the revision link in your delivery email and we'll send an updated plan. Two revisions are included, for 14 days after delivery.
  • A person behind it. Reply to any email we send and a person — Mark — reads it and answers.
03 — What the service is not

Important distinctions. Please read this section.

We are not your attorney, accountant, CPA, tax preparer, financial advisor, or fiduciary. The plan helps you choose software that supports those functions — but the substance of legal, tax, and financial decisions remains with the licensed professionals you already work with (or should hire). We strongly recommend you involve them before making changes that affect money, taxes, or legal matters.

We are not the maker of the tools we recommend. The plan points you to third-party products we don't own or control. Those products have their own terms, pricing, features, and outages — and they change them. Prices and details in your plan are checked when it's written, but they're a snapshot, not a guarantee.

We don't guarantee specific outcomes. We'll tell you honestly what to expect. We won't promise you'll save a specific number of hours or dollars — anyone who does is guessing. And if the honest answer for your situation is "do less than you think, or nothing," the plan will say so.

04 — Pricing & payment

One flat price, no surprises.

  • $79, one time. Paid by card through Stripe before the questionnaire. No subscription, no recurring charge, nothing else from us.
  • One payment, one plan. Each purchase covers one questionnaire and one plan (plus its two included revisions).
  • You pay the tool vendors directly. If a tool the plan recommends has a subscription, you sign up for it and pay the maker — never us. We take no commissions or referral fees. This keeps the recommendations honest.
  • If our price ever changes, it changes for future purchases only — never for a plan you've already bought.
05 — The 14-day refund

Our promise, in writing.

If your plan doesn't feel worth it, we don't want your money. You have 14 days from the moment your plan is delivered to ask for a full refund — no forms, no hoops. Just reply to your delivery email and say the word.

We'll process the refund within five business days, to the card you paid with. The refund covers what you paid us; it doesn't cover third-party tool subscriptions you signed up for directly. Asking for a refund doesn't require an explanation, though we always appreciate hearing what missed.

09 — Limits on liability

The legal part, stated plainly.

We do our work carefully. But software changes, vendors change their pricing and features, and what you do with the plan's recommendations is in your hands. Because of this:

  • Our total liability for any claim arising from the service is limited to what you paid us in the 12 months before the claim.
  • We're not liable for indirect damages — things like lost profits, lost business opportunities, or loss of data in tools we didn't make.
  • Decisions you make based on the plan — including signing up for third-party tools — are yours, made on your own judgment and, where it matters, with your own professional advisors.

None of this limits liability for anything the law doesn't allow us to limit — like intentional misconduct or fraud.
