Lantern Plan — logo directions

Two directions, three wordmark treatments. Same craft level as the current mark (sage rounded square, cream + gold inside, simple geometry). Mark picks one. Nothing here touches the live site until you say so.

Quick note: The metaphor pivots from "two people side by side" (alongside) to "a steady small light to find your way" (lantern). It's a stronger fit for the product — the plan IS the lantern. Audience reads it as warm, guiding, calm, not tech-y.

No mark. Just type. Simpler, faster to ship, harder to use as a favicon or social avatar — you'd need a separate "L" monogram or punctuation mark for those.

  • A or B — wordmark only, or wordmark + lantern mark?
  • Wordmark treatment — italic, caps tag, or equal weight?

My pick (for what it's worth): Direction B (sage square) with italic wordmark. It mirrors the current visual language closely enough that the rename doesn't feel like a totally different company, but the metaphor shift (alongside → lantern) reads cleanly. The italic "Plan" gives the lockup editorial gravity that the caps-tag treatment doesn't — and "Plan" isn't a descriptor like "AI" was, it's part of the name.
